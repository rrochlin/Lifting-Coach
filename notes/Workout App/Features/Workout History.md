## Description
Workout History should show workouts on a calendar view for the history we have. users should be able to click into it to bring them up and view a summary of the exercises done.

## Requirements
- Calendar view of workout history - shown as dots (no details in calendar view)
- Touch interaction brings up a summary of the workout as an overlayed dialog.
- dialog has an edit button to bring it up in an editable dedicated interface similar to workout tracker
- Show one month at a time
